A LITTLE HELP

Private, step by step.

Both people need CipherGap in the same supported web messenger.

  1. 01

    Connect with your partner

    Start from Security. Your partner accepts in the chat. Requests and verification codes expire after 15 minutes.

  2. 02

    Compare the six digits

    Compare over a trusted call or in person, then verify on both sides. A code in the chat alone does not verify identity.

  3. 03

    Send something private

    Use Encrypt for text or Secure files for attachments. The messenger’s ordinary send button sends ordinary text.
